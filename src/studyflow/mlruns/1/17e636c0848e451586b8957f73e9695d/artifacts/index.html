2Probability and Distribution

Concepts of Probability · Probability Clasica and Frequentista · Independence and Conditional Probability

⏱ ~1 min de lectura

Probability and Distribution are the fundamental concepts in statistics that deal with the study of numerical data. The primary objective of these concepts is to describe the characteristics of a dataset without drawing conclusions beyond the data itself. Probability is the measure of the likelihood of an event occurring, and its values range from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain). The distribution of a probability is a way to represent the probability of different outcomes in a dataset. There are various types of distributions, including discrete and continuous distributions, and each has its own characteristics and applications. Understanding probability and distribution is crucial in statistics and data analysis, as it enables us to make informed decisions and draw conclusions from data.

🔑 Conceptos clave

ConceptoDefinición
MeanThe average value of a dataset, calculated by summing all values and dividing by the number of values.
MedianThe middle value of a dataset when it is ordered from smallest to largest. If the dataset has an even number of values, the median is the average of the two middle values.
ModeThe most frequently occurring value in a dataset. A dataset can be unimodal, bimodal, or multimodal, with different modes representing different types of data.
RangeThe difference between the highest and lowest values in a dataset.
VarianceA measure of the spread or dispersion of a dataset from its mean. It is calculated as the average of the squared differences from the mean.
Standard DeviationThe square root of the variance, used to describe the spread of a dataset. It is a measure of the amount of variation or dispersion of a set of values.
HistogramA graphical representation of a dataset, showing the frequency or distribution of different values.
Normal DistributionA continuous probability distribution with a bell-shaped curve, often used to model real-world phenomena. It is characterized by a mean (μ) and standard deviation (σ).
